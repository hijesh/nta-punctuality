Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/09/2026

ÉireRoute ("the app") shows live public transport departure times for Ireland. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Location data

If you use the "Stops near me" or walking-time features, the app asks your browser or device for your current location. This is used only to:

  • Find bus/rail stops near you
  • Calculate walking time from your location to a stop, via a third-party routing service (OpenRouteService)

Your coordinates are sent to our server to perform these calculations and are not permanently stored or linked to your identity. They are also sent to OpenRouteService solely to calculate a walking route; no other identifying information is sent along with them.

Anonymous usage analytics

The app assigns your browser a random, anonymous identifier (stored as a cookie) to count visits and see which stops are looked up most often. This does not include your name, email, exact location, or any other personal information - only that "some anonymous visitor" viewed a page or stop. This data is used solely to understand how the app is used and improve it.

Feedback you submit

If you use the in-app feedback button, the message you write is sent to the developer, along with the anonymous visitor identifier above and, if applicable, which stop you were viewing at the time. Please don't include personal information in feedback messages.

Data we don't collect

  • No account or sign-up is required or offered
  • No name, email address, or phone number is collected
  • No data is sold to third parties or used for advertising

Data stored on your device only

Your saved/favourite stops, theme preference (light/dark), and alert settings are stored locally in your browser or device and are never sent to our server. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes this information permanently.

Third-party services used

  • National Transport Authority (NTA) - provides the live departure and schedule data this app displays. See nationaltransport.ie.
  • OpenRouteService - calculates walking routes/times when you use that feature.

Data retention

Analytics and feedback data may be periodically cleared as part of routine server maintenance and are not retained indefinitely.
